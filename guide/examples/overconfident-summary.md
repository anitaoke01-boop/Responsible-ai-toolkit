# Illustrative example: overconfident summary

**All scenario details below are synthetic and illustrative.**

## Scenario

An AI assistant summarizes fictional feedback from six volunteers after a trial of a new meeting template.

## Flawed output

> “The new template improves meeting productivity across the organization.”

The small volunteer group and informal comments cannot establish organization-wide improvement or cause.

## How the reviewer catches it

The reviewer compares the summary with the six comments and notices there is no comparison group, agreed productivity measure, or representative sample. Two comments mention extra preparation time.

## Corrected approach

Write: “In this small illustrative volunteer trial, four of six respondents said the template helped them prepare. Two noted extra preparation time. These comments do not establish an organization-wide effect; a broader evaluation would be needed.”
