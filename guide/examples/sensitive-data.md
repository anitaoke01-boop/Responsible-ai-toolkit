# Illustrative example: sensitive data pasted into a tool

**All scenario details below are synthetic and illustrative.**

## Scenario

A staff member asks an unapproved AI tool to draft a response from a fictional customer complaint and pastes the full case record, including a name, account number, and health detail.

## Flawed output

> “Dear Mira Vale, account 000-123, regarding your treatment note…”

The draft repeats sensitive details and the record was shared with a tool not approved for that data.

## How the reviewer catches it

Before sending, a supervisor compares the prompt and draft with the approved-tool list and sees identifying and health information in both. The draft also repeats details unnecessary to answer the request.

## Corrected approach

Stop using the unapproved tool for this case. Follow the organization’s incident-reporting process for the accidental disclosure. For future drafting, use an approved workflow and only the minimum necessary information; use a clearly synthetic case for practice. A human checks the response against the authorized case record before sending.
