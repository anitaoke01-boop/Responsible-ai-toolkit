# AI-assisted work: one-page verification checklist

Use this before approving, sharing, or acting on work that used AI. The examples and sample content in this project are synthetic.

## Before using the tool

- [ ] The task is allowed by current workplace rules and any applicable requirements.
- [ ] The tool is approved for this purpose and information type.
- [ ] The prompt and files contain no secrets, credentials, personal records, or confidential details unless explicitly approved.
- [ ] Hidden sheets, comments, notes, tracked changes, metadata, and attachments have been checked.
- [ ] The task has a named human owner and a clear intended use.

## Check the result

- [ ] It answers the actual request and follows the supplied source material.
- [ ] Important claims have sources I opened and checked for relevance, date, and support.
- [ ] Quotes, names, dates, and citations match the original source.
- [ ] Important figures have correct units, period, denominator, filters, and arithmetic.
- [ ] Exceptions, limitations, conflicting evidence, and missing context are represented.
- [ ] The output does not add unsupported facts or present assumptions as facts.
- [ ] I considered who may be omitted or harmed and whether standards are applied consistently.
- [ ] I checked whether the wording sounds more certain than the evidence allows.

## Before approval

- [ ] Review effort matches the impact; a qualified person has reviewed high-impact work.
- [ ] I corrected, qualified, removed, or escalated anything I could not verify.
- [ ] The final text is understandable, appropriately attributed, and not misleading about AI use.
- [ ] Required sources, assumptions, changes, disclosures, and approvals are recorded.
- [ ] I can explain why the final result is reliable enough for its intended use.

**If a critical check fails:** pause. Do not approve or rely on the output until the issue is resolved through an approved process.
